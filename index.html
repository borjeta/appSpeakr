Speakr (English)

Speakr is a public speaking coach and interview simulator for iPhone. Record your presentations or answer a virtual interviewer and get a report on your filler words, pace, pauses, intonation, eye contact and content. Everything is analyzed on your iPhone, with no accounts and no servers.
