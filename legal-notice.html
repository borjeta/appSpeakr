2. Purpose of the app

Speakr is a mobile application for iPhone (iOS 26 or later) that works as a public speaking coach and interview simulator: it lets you record presentations and interview answers and get an automatic report (filler words, pace, pauses, intonation, orientation of the face toward the camera and an assessment of the content), and practice with a virtual interviewer that asks questions and follow-up questions. The analysis is performed on the user's device. It is available in Spanish and English.

Using the app implies acceptance of these terms.

3. Terms of use

3.1. Practice tool; guidance only

The app's scores, metrics, tips and assessments are generated automatically (with calculation rules and, if your iPhone supports it, with Apple Intelligence). They are for guidance and general in nature, may contain errors and do not guarantee any result in a real presentation, interview or selection process. They do not replace a coach, trainer or qualified professional. Speakr is not a recruitment or candidate-assessment tool.

3.2. Estimates and technical limits

The transcript may contain speech-recognition errors, which affects the count of filler words and other metrics. “Eye contact” is an approximate estimate of the orientation of the face, not of the direction of the eyes. Feedback generated with artificial intelligence may be inaccurate or inappropriate. Do not make important decisions based exclusively on these results.
