2. Summary

  • The app has no user accounts, has no servers of its own, includes no usage analytics (only the purchase statistics that RevenueCat provides, section 7), shows no advertising and does not track you: it does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not ask you for Apple's “Tracking” permission, because it does not track.
  • Speakr analyzes your practice sessions on your iPhone. Transcription, voice metrics (filler words, pace, pauses, intonation), the estimate of whether you look at the camera and the content feedback are all computed on the device itself (section 5). Your recordings, transcripts and reports are stored only on your iPhone: the controller does not receive them and cannot see them.
  • By default only the audio is kept for each practice session. Video is kept only if you turn on “Keep the video” in Settings. In both cases, recordings are excluded from backups (section 11).
  • Your facial data is not stored. Of the estimate of whether your face is turned toward the camera, only a percentage remains in the report. The app does not identify or recognize anyone (section 5.3).
  • The only network communication the app itself makes is with RevenueCat, to manage the optional Speakr Pro subscription. Apple processes the payment and your Apple ID (the app never sees them) and RevenueCat, a third party acting as the controller's processor, validates and restores the subscription using an anonymous identifier, your purchase history, your IP address and technical data about the device and the app. It is not used for tracking or advertising. Details in section 6.
  • The first time you practice in a language, iOS (not the app) may download that language's speech model from Apple. This is a system download and does not send your recordings (section 5.1).
  • You can delete your practice sessions from the Progress tab (which also deletes their recording) or by uninstalling the app (section 8). Data that Apple or RevenueCat already hold is not on your device (section 6).
  • The free plan and Speakr Pro do not change how your data is handled: what changes are the usage limits (section 6.1).

5. On-device processing

5.1. Transcription

Transcription is done by Apple's speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer) on your iPhone. The first time you practice in a language (Spanish or English), iOS may download that language's model from Apple. The download is performed by the operating system, is governed by Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/), and, as with any download, Apple sees the device's IP address; none of your recordings or text is sent.

5.2. Voice metrics

Filler words, pace, pauses, intonation and volume variation are calculated inside the app, on your iPhone, from the audio and the transcript. No servers are involved.

5.3. Eye-contact estimate

  • If “Analyze camera facing” is on and there is video, the app takes frames from the recorded video (about four per second, in memory only, without saving them) and Apple's Vision framework, on your iPhone, estimates whether the face is facing the camera (head turn and tilt within about 15°).
  • It is an approximate estimate: it measures the orientation of the face, not the direction of the eyes.
  • Only a percentage is saved in the report. No images of the face, facial maps or templates are stored, and the app does not identify or recognize people.
  • You can turn off “Analyze camera facing” in Settings (it applies to presentation practice sessions and interviews).
  • If you turn on “Keep the video”, the video file, which contains your image, is kept on your iPhone like any other data in section 3. It does not leave the device.

5.4. Content feedback (Apple Intelligence)

If your iPhone has Apple Intelligence available, the app uses the system language model, which runs on the device, to assess the content of your practice. It is given the text of your transcript (summarized in chunks if it is long) and the topic you typed; in interviews, also the question and an excerpt of the job posting you pasted. Speakr does not send that text to any server. If Apple Intelligence is not available, the app assesses only form and structure using rules calculated on your iPhone.

5.5. Interviewer's voice

In the interview simulator, the questions are read aloud with the system's speech synthesis voices (AVSpeechSynthesizer), on your iPhone.

5.6. External links

The links to the Terms of Use (Apple's standard license), to this privacy policy and to the support page, on the Speakr Pro screen and in Settings, open websites in your device's browser (Apple's and this site's), which communicates with those sites under their own terms. The app does not send any data about you when you open them.

6. Subscription (Apple App Store and RevenueCat)

6.1. What is offered

Speakr is free with limits (3 practice sessions per week and 1 interview) and offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, “Speakr Pro”, monthly or annual (the annual one with a free trial), which removes those limits. You do not need to subscribe to use the free part. The price and the conditions of the free trial are shown by the App Store itself before you confirm. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; it is managed and cancelled in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

6.2. Who does what

  • Apple charges and manages the payment, your Apple ID and refunds. The app does not see or store your payment data (card, payment method, billing name). Apple processes that data under its own policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (a third-party service, with servers in the US) acts as the controller's processor: it verifies with Apple that the subscription is genuine and restores it when you ask. According to its policy, RevenueCat is not the controller of this end-user data and acts as a processor; in its DPA, the customer (here, the controller) is the data controller and RevenueCat its processor. Its privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/. Its data processing agreement (DPA): https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa/.
  • The controller (section 1) decides to use RevenueCat for this function. It does not receive your payment data and cannot tell who you are from the anonymous identifier.

6.4. Retention

The controller does not store this data on any server of its own. RevenueCat keeps it according to its policy and its DPA: its policy states that, for end-user data, it keeps it for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce its agreements; and under the DPA, for the duration of its contract with the controller, after which it destroys or returns the data if the controller so requests. The controller may ask RevenueCat to delete a record. Apple keeps the data on your purchases according to its policy. The subscription itself belongs to your Apple ID, not to the app.

6.5. How to restore the subscription

If you have already subscribed (for example, after reinstalling the app or switching iPhones), tap “Restore purchases” in Settings, or “Restore” on the Speakr Pro screen, and sign in, if asked, with the same Apple ID you subscribed with. There is no app account: the subscription is tied to your Apple ID and is activated again. An Internet connection is required. Deleting your practice sessions does not cancel the subscription, and neither does uninstalling the app: it is cancelled in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

6.6. Your rights over the subscription data

Because there are no accounts, the controller cannot link the anonymous identifier to you on its own. If you want to access this data or request its deletion, write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com; to locate your record at RevenueCat, the anonymous identifier of your installation or details of your purchase (for example, Apple's receipt) may be needed. The controller will handle the request with RevenueCat. Note that, if your record at RevenueCat is deleted, the subscription will still exist on your Apple ID and can be restored again. You may lodge a complaint with the AEPD (section 12).

6.7. Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the controller or the app. Request them at https://reportaproblem.apple.com (see Support and the Legal notice).

7. International transfers

The controller does not transfer personal data to third countries by itself, because it does not receive your recordings, transcripts or reports. The only transfers are those by third parties in the context of the subscription:

  • RevenueCat stores data on Amazon Web Services servers in the US, according to its privacy policy. For transfers of personal data from the European Economic Area to countries without an adequacy decision, its DPA relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) (with a UK addendum and Swiss modifications) and lists its sub-processors (all in the US, including AWS).
  • Apple processes payment data, your Apple ID data and model downloads according to its policy, also outside the European Economic Area. We have no control over those transfers.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device (section 3): kept until you delete it.
  • Deleting a practice session: in the Progress tab, swipe the session to the left and tap the delete button. The session (report, transcript and interview data) is removed together with its audio or video recording.
  • Turning off “Keep the video” only affects future sessions: videos already saved are removed by deleting the session.
  • Deleting everything: in Settings > “Delete all my data” every practice session and every stored recording on the device is deleted (it asks for confirmation and cannot be undone). Uninstalling the app also removes the settings. Keep iOS backups in mind (section 11).
  • Temporary files: while a practice session is being analyzed, the recording is in a temporary folder of the app; the app deletes it or turns it into the saved audio or video, and any leftovers from interrupted sessions are handled by the operating system like any temporary file.
  • Data at Apple and RevenueCat: it is not on your device and is not deleted when you delete your sessions or uninstall (section 6.4). To request its deletion, see section 6.6.
  • Data held by the controller: the controller does not keep any of your app data. If you write by email, it will process your address and the content of your message only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in handling your enquiry) and will keep them for as long as needed for that and to meet its legal obligations.

9. What does NOT happen

  • There are no accounts, registration or sign-in.
  • There is no analytics of its own, no advertising, no ad networks, and no tracking across other companies' apps and websites. The app does not use the advertising identifier or ask for the “Tracking” permission.
  • The app does not upload or share your recordings, videos, transcripts or reports with any server or third party, and has no function of its own to do so. They are not used to train artificial intelligence models: the controller does not receive them.
  • No facial data is stored: no images of your face, no facial maps or templates. No one is identified or recognized.
  • The app does not use your location, your contacts or your photos.
  • No error reports are sent to a server of the controller. (The controller can only receive, anonymously, the crash reports that Apple provides to developers if you have turned on the option to share them in iOS.)
  • The app does not see or store your payment data: Apple handles it.

10. General legal basis, automated decisions and minors

10.1. Legal basis (for guidance)

The General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the applicable Spanish law only regulate the processing of personal data. For guidance:

  • Data stored only on your device: the controller does not receive it or access it, so it does not process it as a controller. You control that data and can delete it whenever you wish. The use of the camera, the microphone and speech recognition depends on the permissions you grant (section 4), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Subscription (Apple and RevenueCat): see section 6.3. RevenueCat's processing is carried out on behalf of the controller, as processor.
  • International transfers: see section 7.
  • Automated decisions and profiling: the controller does not carry them out. Scores, metrics and tips are calculated by your own iPhone to help you practice; the controller does not receive them and they produce no legal effects on you. Speakr is not a recruitment or candidate-screening tool.

10.2. Minors

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not offer it as a children's app. Nor should anyone under 14 (the minimum age to consent to data processing in Spain) use it without the authorization of their parents or guardians. The app does not ask for age or personal data, has no accounts and shows no advertising. Subscriptions are handled by Apple with the controls configured on the Apple ID and the device (for example, “Ask to Buy” in Family Sharing). If you believe a minor has used the app without the necessary authorization, write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com.

11. System backups

  • Recordings: the app flags the recordings folder (Documents/Recordings) to be excluded from iCloud and computer backups, whether you keep audio only or video.
  • Practice sessions and settings: the rest of the local data (the database with your reports, transcripts and the job posting you pasted, and the settings) may be included in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your device's configuration. That is handled by Apple and you, not by the controller. If you restore an iPhone from a backup, the practice sessions may come back, but without their recording. If you do not want them included, check your device's backup settings or delete the sessions beforehand (section 8).
  • The subscription does not depend on these backups: it is recovered with “Restore purchases” (section 6.5).

12. Your rights and the supervisory authority

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing, portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Because the controller does not receive your practice data, the most direct way to exercise those rights is to use the app itself (everything is on your device and you can view and delete it; you can withdraw permissions in iOS settings). For the subscription data processed by RevenueCat as the controller's processor, write to the controller (section 6.6); payment data and your Apple ID are handled by Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/). If you have questions or want to exercise any right with respect to the controller, write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com.

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD): https://www.aepd.es, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

In particular, if a feature were ever added that sent your transcripts or recordings to a server (for example, a cloud content coach), your explicit consent would be requested before you use it and this policy would be updated. The current version is always the one published at https://borjeta.github.io/appSpeakr/privacy.html.
