Speakr Support

This page is the official support for the Speakr app, published by Borja Alventosa Julià. Last update: November 18, 2026. Versión en español.

What is Speakr

Speakr is a public speaking coach and interview simulator for iPhone. In Presentation mode you record yourself talking about a topic and get a report on your filler words, pace, pauses, intonation, eye contact (approximate) and content. In the interview simulator, a virtual interviewer asks you questions, follows up if your answer falls short and evaluates each answer with the STAR method. It has no accounts and no servers of its own, and everything is analyzed on your iPhone. It is free with limits and offers an optional subscription, Speakr Pro.

Speakr is a practice tool: scores and tips are for guidance only and do not guarantee any result in a real presentation or interview.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use Speakr?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later, with a camera and a microphone. You can practice in Spanish and English. Content feedback additionally requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (Apple publishes the list of compatible models and languages) with that feature turned on; without it, Speakr still works and assesses the form and structure of what you say (see below).

Does it work without an Internet connection?

You do not need a connection to practice and get your report: everything is analyzed on the iPhone. The exception is the first time you practice in a language: iOS may need to download that language's speech model from Apple, so it is best to have a connection and free space. The subscription does need a connection to view the plans, subscribe and restore, and the app checks its status when you open it.

What permissions does the app ask for and why?

  • Camera: preview and recording of your practice; on the video, the app estimates, approximately, whether your face is turned toward the camera.
  • Microphone: recording your voice. It is required to practice.
  • Speech recognition: transcribing your practice on the iPhone. If iOS asks for it, it is used only for that.

The app does not use your location, contacts or photos, and does not track you. You can change any permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security (or on Speakr's page within Settings): if you denied the microphone, turn it on there so you can record. Full details in the privacy policy.

Are my recordings sent anywhere?

No. The recording, transcript, metrics and feedback are computed on your iPhone and stored only on your iPhone. The controller does not receive them. The only network communication the app makes is with RevenueCat for the subscription (see below).

Where are my recordings stored and how much space do they take?

Only on your iPhone. By default only the audio is kept for each practice session (about 1 MB per minute). If you also want to keep the video, turn on “Keep the video” in the Settings > Analysis tab (it takes about 40 MB per minute). Recordings are not included in iCloud backups.

What is “camera facing”? Is it exact?

It is an approximate estimate: Speakr measures whether your face is turned toward the camera, not where your eyes are looking. Only a percentage is saved in the report from that analysis; no images or data of your face are stored. You can turn it off in Settings > Analysis > Analyze eye contact (it applies to presentation practice sessions).

Why am I not getting content feedback?

Content feedback (key message, structure, clarity, suggestions) is generated by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, and is only available if your device supports it and you have it turned on. In Settings > Analysis you can see whether it is available. If it is not, Speakr analyzes only form (filler words, pace, pauses, intonation) and the structure of your answers using rules.

The app says the iPhone cannot transcribe in a language, or transcription fails

Check that you have an Internet connection and free space (the first time, iOS downloads the language's speech model) and try again. If the problem continues, write to us stating the language, your iPhone model and your iOS version.

How do I delete my data?

  • One practice session: in the Progress tab, swipe the session to the left and tap the delete button. The report, the transcript and also its audio or video recording are removed.
  • Everything: tap Settings > “Delete all my data” (it asks for confirmation). If you uninstall the app, the settings are removed from the device as well.
  • Turning off “Keep the video” does not delete videos already saved: delete them by deleting the corresponding session.
  • Keep in mind that iCloud or computer backups may contain the reports and transcripts; check them in your device's settings. More detail in the privacy policy, sections 8 and 11.

What does the free plan include and what does Speakr Pro add?

Free: 3 practice sessions per week and 1 interview. With Speakr Pro you do not have those limits. The free plan and Speakr Pro handle your data in the same way.

How much does Speakr Pro cost?

Speakr Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or annual (the annual one with a free trial). The price and the trial conditions are shown by the App Store on the Speakr Pro screen, in your currency, before you confirm.

How do I subscribe and how does the free trial work?

Tap “Unlock Speakr Pro” in the Settings tab (or the prompt that appears when you reach the free limit), choose the plan and confirm with your Apple ID. The annual subscription includes a free trial; its length and the date of the first charge are shown on the purchase screen. To avoid being charged when the trial ends, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

How do I cancel the subscription?

The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Cancel it in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Speakr. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription. You will keep Speakr Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap “Restore purchases” in the Settings tab, or “Restore” on the Speakr Pro screen. Sign in, if asked, with the same Apple ID you subscribed with; an Internet connection is required. If it does not appear, check that you are using the right Apple ID and write to us with Apple's receipt (the “Apple receipt” email).

I switched iPhones or reinstalled the app: do I lose Speakr Pro? And my practice sessions?

You do not lose Speakr Pro: the subscription belongs to your Apple ID. On the new iPhone, install the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Your practice sessions, however, are only on the old iPhone: the app has no accounts, so they cannot be recovered when reinstalling (if you restore the iPhone from a backup, the reports may come back, but not the recordings).

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer, and the developer cannot issue them directly. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com (sign in with your Apple ID, choose the option to request a refund, the reason and the purchase). Apple decides. If you are a consumer in the EU, your statutory rights are not limited: see the Legal notice, section 5.

What data does the subscription use?

Apple processes the payment (the app does not see your payment data). RevenueCat, a third-party service acting as the developer's processor, validates and restores the subscription using an anonymous identifier of the app, your purchase history, the IP address and technical data about your device and the app. There is no account and no tracking. Details in the privacy policy, section 6.

How do I report a bug or send a suggestion?

Write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com stating: iPhone model, iOS version, app version, what you were doing and what you expected to happen. Do not attach recordings or sensitive data unless essential. The app does not automatically send error reports to the controller, so we will not know about the problem unless you tell us.
