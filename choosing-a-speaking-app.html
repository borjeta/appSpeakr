How to choose a public speaking practice app: 7 criteria

There are many apps for practicing public speaking and job interviews. These seven criteria help you compare them using concrete facts, Speakr included.

The 7 criteria

  1. What it really analyzes. Filler words, pace, pauses, intonation, content? Check whether it gives concrete metrics or only a general opinion.
  2. Where your voice is processed. On the device or on the developer's servers. Look at what data leaves the phone and read the privacy policy.
  3. Whether it requires an account. An account adds personal data and one more screen; is it necessary?
  4. Practice languages. An app being available in your language does not mean it analyzes what you say well in that language. Check which languages you can speak in.
  5. Pricing model. Free plan, limits, subscription, free trial and auto-renewal. Read the terms before you confirm.
  6. Mock interviews. If you are preparing for interviews, look for questions by category, an assessment of your answer's structure (for example STAR) and follow-up questions.
  7. Honesty about limits. Automatic metrics are estimates. Be wary of apps that promise sure results.

How Speakr fits

  • Where it is processed: on the iPhone. Speakr does not send recordings or transcripts to any server; the only network communication the app itself makes is with RevenueCat, for the subscription.
  • Account: no accounts and no ads.
  • Languages: app and practice in English and Spanish.
  • Price: a free plan with limits (3 presentation practices every 7 days and 1 interview) and an optional Speakr Pro subscription: €4.99 per month or €34.99 per year, with a 7-day trial on the annual plan for eligible new subscribers.
  • Interviews: virtual interviewer, 3 to 8 questions per interview, STAR assessment, numbers, first person and one follow-up question.

  • Content feedback requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence.
