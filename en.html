Speakr: practice public speaking and job interviews

Speakr is an iPhone app that trains public speaking and simulates job interviews. You record yourself speaking; the app analyzes your filler words, pace, pauses, intonation and approximate camera facing on your iPhone, and gives you a report with a score from 0 to 100 and your top 3 priorities. It works in Spanish and English, with no accounts and no ads.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Two ways to practice

Presentation

Pick 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes, add a topic if you like, and start talking. When you finish, you get a report with:

  • Filler words: an estimate of how many you use, highlighted in the transcript.
  • Pace: your words per minute over time, against a target range.
  • Pauses: when and for how long you stop.
  • Intonation: pitch variation and monotone stretches.
  • Language confidence: hedging phrases, repetitions and crutch words.
  • Camera facing (approximate): estimated from the orientation of your face; it is not eye tracking.
  • Content (with Apple Intelligence, on a supported iPhone): key message, clarity and suggestions.

Job interview simulator

A virtual HR interviewer reads each question out loud and you answer by recording yourself. Speakr assesses your answer with the STAR method (situation, task, action, result), checks whether your results include numbers and how much you speak in the first person versus "we", and asks one follow-up question if the result or your own contribution is missing. Each interview has 3 to 8 questions (opening, motivation, behavioral and closing) and you can paste the job posting so the assessment takes it into account (with Apple Intelligence).

How it works

  1. Choose Presentation or the interview simulator, the language you will speak in and the length.
  2. Speak. The app records audio and, if you allow it, video with the front camera.
  3. Get your report: a score from 0 to 100, 3 priorities with one tip each, 2 strengths, metrics, a pace chart and a transcript. Your practices are saved so you can see your progress.

Privacy

  • Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition and content feedback uses Apple Intelligence, both on your iPhone.
  • No accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own.
  • Speakr never sends your recordings or transcripts to any server. The only network communication the app itself makes is with RevenueCat, to manage the optional subscription.
  • By default only the audio is kept; video is kept only if you turn on "Keep the video" in Settings. You can delete a practice, or all your data, from the app.

Frequently asked questions

What is Speakr?

Speakr is an iPhone app (iOS 26 or later) for practicing public speaking and job interviews. You record yourself, the app analyzes the recording on your iPhone, and you get a report with a score from 0 to 100, your top 3 priorities and concrete tips.

What does Speakr analyze when I speak?

It estimates your filler words, pace (words per minute), pauses, intonation and language confidence (hedging phrases, repetitions and crutch words). If there is video, it makes a rough estimate of whether your face is turned toward the camera. With Apple Intelligence, on a supported iPhone, it also assesses content: key message, clarity and suggestions.

Are my recordings sent to a server?

No. The recording, transcript, metrics and feedback are computed and stored only on your iPhone. The only network communication the app itself makes is with RevenueCat, to manage the optional subscription. The first time you practice in a language, iOS may download that language's speech model from Apple.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Speakr has no accounts and no ads. A subscription, if you buy one, is tied to your Apple ID and is recovered with “Restore purchases”.

Which languages can I practice in?

Spanish and English. The app is available in both languages and you choose which one you will speak in each practice. The interview simulator's question bank is also in Spanish and English.

Is it free?

The download is free. The free plan includes 3 presentation practices every 7 days and 1 mock interview. Speakr Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes those limits: €4.99 per month or €34.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan for eligible new subscribers (prices for Spain).

Does it track my eyes or measure eye contact?

No. Speakr makes a rough estimate of whether your face is turned toward the camera (head turn and tilt); it does not measure where your eyes are looking. Only a percentage is saved in the report, and you can turn this estimate off in Settings.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later and a microphone (required). The camera is optional: without it, Speakr records audio only. Content feedback needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence with the feature turned on; without it, Speakr analyzes the form and structure of what you say.

Does it work offline?

You do not need a connection to practice and get your report, except the first time you practice in a language (iOS may need to download the speech model). The subscription does need a connection to view plans, buy and restore.

Does it guarantee I will improve or get hired?

No. Speakr is a practice tool: its metrics are rough estimates and do not guarantee results in a real presentation or interview.
